These weights control how the morning brief decides BUY / SELL / HOLD. Buffett's learning engine adjusts them automatically over time, but you can override them here.

How the Decision Works

1

Compute the Signal Score

Signal = Tech Score + Fund Score × Fundamental Trust × 0.35 + Commodity Signal × Commodity Weight

Every morning, the system scores the stock's technicals (MA, MACD, RSI, KDJ, Bollinger, volume) on a scale of −100 to +100. Fundamentals (growth, valuation, profitability) are scored separately. The Fundamental Trust weight controls how much the fundamental score blends into the final signal. The Commodity Signal (−15 to +15) reflects SHFE nickel and lithium carbonate price momentum — when upstream commodity prices rally, it boosts the buy signal since Huayou's margins improve. Set Commodity Weight higher to let commodity price movements influence decisions more.

2

Compare Against Thresholds

Signal ≥ BUY Threshold → BUY (aggressive)
Signal ≥ Light BUY Threshold → BUY (light)
Between the two thresholds → HOLD
Signal ≤ Light SELL Threshold → SELL (trim)
Signal ≤ SELL Threshold → SELL (reduce)

The four thresholds define five zones on the signal scale. Raising the BUY thresholds makes Buffett pickier about buying (fewer, higher-conviction entries). Lowering the SELL thresholds (more negative) means Buffett only sells in more extreme downturns.

3

Compute Confidence

Confidence = Base Confidence × Regime Trust + Expert Influence

Base confidence comes from how well the current market pattern matches historical outcomes. Regime Trust scales this up or down — set >1.0 if you believe history repeats, <1.0 if you think "this time is different." Expert Influence is the boost (or penalty) applied when TaoGuBa experts agree (or disagree) with the technical direction. A value of 0.08 means ±8% confidence shift.

4

Regime-Aware Dynamic Strategy

Instead of using one set of rules for all conditions, Buffett detects the current market regime (trend + RSI + volatility + momentum + streak) and activates a matching strategy. Each strategy adjusts thresholds and position sizing automatically:

Trend Following (uptrend + momentum) Easier BUY, tighter SELL — ride the wave (70% position)
Swing / Range-Bound (sideways) Standard thresholds — buy support, sell resistance (50% position)
Defensive / Exit (downtrend + high vol) Harder BUY, easier SELL — protect capital (20% position)
Contrarian Bounce (down + oversold) Easier BUY, block SELL — catch the reversal (30% position)
Capitulation Watch (≥5 down days or -10%) Very easy BUY on any signal — snap-back likely (25% position)

The strategy mode is shown at the top of each morning brief. Position percentages are advisory — how much of your swing allocation to deploy in this regime.

5

Safety Overrides

Even after the formula and regime strategy run, Buffett applies safety checks: overbought RSI blocks aggressive BUY; oversold RSI blocks SELL; very poor fundamentals downgrade aggressive BUY to light BUY. These are hardcoded guardrails you cannot override here.

How the Learning Engine Works

Every time the morning brief runs, Buffett looks back at past signals and checks whether they were right or wrong. Based on the win rate, it nudges the weights up or down in small steps. You never have to touch anything — but if you disagree with a direction, you can override below.

1

Score Each Past Signal

For every past brief, the engine checks what actually happened 1 day and 5 days later (blended 60/40). It classifies each signal as:

Correct — BUY and price went up (net of fees), or SELL and price dropped, or HOLD and market was flat
Wrong — the opposite happened
Neutral — HOLD and the market moved moderately (inconclusive)
2

Adjust Weights Based on Win Rate

The engine looks at the win rate for BUY calls, SELL calls, and overall accuracy, then nudges the corresponding weights. The rules are simple and conservative:

BUY win rate < 40% → Raise BUY Threshold (be pickier about buying)
BUY win rate > 70% → Lower BUY Threshold (buy more often since it’s working)
SELL win rate < 40% → Lower SELL Threshold (require stronger bearish signal to sell)
SELL win rate > 70% → Raise SELL Threshold (sell more readily since it’s working)
Overall win rate < 45% → Reduce Regime Trust & Expert Influence (trust patterns less)
Overall win rate > 65% → Increase Regime Trust & Expert Influence (trust them more)

Between 40–70% (BUY/SELL) and 45–65% (overall), no changes are made — the weights stay put. All adjustments are small incremental steps, clamped within the min/max range shown on each card below. Needs at least {{ min_samples }} briefs before any learning kicks in.

3

Per-Regime Fine-Tuning

Beyond the global weights, the engine also tracks accuracy per market regime (e.g., “uptrend + neutral RSI” vs. “downtrend + overbought RSI”). If BUY calls keep failing in a specific regime, the threshold gets raised just for that regime. These per-regime adjustments are not shown on this page but are applied automatically.
